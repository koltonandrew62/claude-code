What do these numbers mean?

Outlook runs from −100 (bearish) to +100 (bullish). It blends a technical score, a fundamental score and a news score.

Catalyst runs from 0 to 100 and shows how strongly big-event signals are firing. M&A, FDA, Earnings and Event are the signals behind it.

News is the tone of recent headlines, −100 to +100. 60 days is the recent price trend.

Tap a ranking to re-sort, or any row for the full picture: price ranges with confidence levels, earnings, filings and option strikes. None of this is investment advice.
